04 — Transformation Framework

The Operations
Transformation Roadmap

A universal framework for taking any operation from reactive to intelligent — applicable across manufacturing, supply chain, service operations, and logistics.

L0
Reactive
Decisions driven by experience, not evidence. Processes documented on paper, issues addressed after they occur.
L1
Structured
Basic digitization, standardized processes, KPI tracking. Data exists but lives in silos — the foundation for everything that follows.
L2
Connected ACTIVE
Systems communicate with each other. Real-time visibility across operations. Data flows from execution layer to management layer.
L3
Analytical
Historical and predictive analytics shape decisions. Patterns become insights. Optimization shifts from reactive to proactive.
L4
Intelligent
AI surfaces anomalies, suggests actions, automates analysis. The organization starts to reason about itself in real time.
L5
Adaptive
Self-optimizing, closed-loop operations. Humans set direction and strategy; intelligent systems execute, monitor, and refine.
📊
Where I've Applied This

Driving the L1→L2 transition in European manufacturing operations — implementing digital reporting, real-time KPI visibility, and data-informed decision-making. The same framework applies whether you're running a factory, a service operation, or a logistics network.
